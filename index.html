About RP Restaurant

A Place to Eat, Relax & Come Back To

RP Restaurant is a welcoming dining destination in Phaphamau, Prayagraj, created around good food, comfortable dining and warm hospitality.

From North Indian favourites to Chinese classics, the restaurant offers a vegetarian dining experience suited to family meals, casual dining, groups and travellers passing through the area.
